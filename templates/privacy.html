Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1Data We Collect

We collect only what is needed to run Spendly:

  • Account details, such as your name, email address and password (stored in hashed form).
  • Expense data you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.
  • Basic technical information, such as browser type and log data, used to keep the service working.

2How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide and improve Spendly: showing your expenses, building category breakdowns and summaries, keeping your account secure, and responding to your requests. We do not sell your personal data or use your expense records for advertising.

3Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and is accessible only to you and to the systems needed to run the service. We keep it for as long as your account is active. You can delete your expenses or your account at any time, after which your data is removed from our systems within a reasonable period.

4Third Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of third party services, such as Google Fonts to load typefaces. These providers may receive technical information like your IP address when your browser requests their resources. We do not share your expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to, or deletion of, your data, email us at support@spendly.example.
